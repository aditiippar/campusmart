How it works

Buying

  1. Browse the marketplace
  2. Search and apply filters
  3. Open a product, check condition and price
  4. Chat with the seller
  5. Agree a meeting place and time
  6. Inspect the item
  7. Pay in person
  8. Update the order status

Selling

  1. Create an account
  2. Open Sell and upload photos
  3. Add title, description, category, condition, price
  4. Publish
  5. Reply to buyers
  6. Meet safely and hand over the item

Safety

Meet in public campus spots, inspect before paying, never share passwords or OTPs, and use Report on any product that looks suspicious.

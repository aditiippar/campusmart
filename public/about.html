💜 About us

Made by a student, for students

CampusMart is a campus marketplace where students buy, sell and reuse textbooks, notes, calculators and other study essentials, directly with each other.

Our mission & vision

Mission

Make study materials affordable and easy to find by connecting students on the same campus, with no middlemen and no hidden fees.

Vision

A campus where nothing useful goes to waste: every book, instrument and set of notes finds its next student.

What we stand for

Affordable

Study materials at student-friendly prices.

Sustainable

Every book reused is one less thrown away.

Direct

Real-time chat between buyer and seller.

Safe

Reports, moderation and meet-on-campus handovers.
